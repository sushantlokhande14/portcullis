# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not the person who wrote
it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the change, with a reason. "This will break on Windows
  because paths are not UTF-8" is useful; "this is wrong" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way. A review comment is about the diff.
- Remember that contributors have different levels of experience, different
  first languages, and different amounts of time. A question that seems obvious
  to you was not obvious to the person asking.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwelcome attention, and
publishing others' private information. Deliberately wasting maintainer or
contributor time also falls here.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

Report a problem to sushantlokhande094@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.
The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this
document, and may block accounts, with an explanation given where it is safe to
do so.
